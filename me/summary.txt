My technical toolkit includes Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Node.js, C#, MongoDB, SQL and PostgreSQL. I have also worked with REST APIs, authentication, cloud services, payments and modern frontend tooling.

## How I work

I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration with a focus on marketing. I have used that perspective throughout my visual merchandising work: understanding audiences, building coherent brand experiences and translating commercial goals into practical choices.

Before moving into software development, I built around 15 years of experience in customer service, sales and visual merchandising, including approximately five years in leadership roles. I have worked with customers, coached teams and helped create commercial spaces and experiences in fast-moving retail environments.

Visual merchandising is both creative and highly practical work. It has taught me to generate ideas quickly, plan details carefully and make confident decisions when situations change. I am used to balancing a clear visual direction with real-world constraints, customer needs and tight deadlines.

As a developer, I bring the same marketing mindset into product work. I consider not only how something is built, but also who it is for, what it needs to communicate and how it supports a meaningful user experience. I bring curiosity, thoughtful planning, ownership and a strong focus on the people using the product.
